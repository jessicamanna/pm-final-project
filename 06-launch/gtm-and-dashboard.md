## Go-to-market summary

_A concise overview of your launch plan: channels, launch size, and overall strategy._

We will launch Nomi's Smart Brief (one chat to a compliance-reviewed, on-brand product flyer in about 3 minutes) to independent retail mortgage lenders with a conversion goal: turn the pilot lender's results into 5 signed lenders within 90 days of launch. The audience is the person who signs at the lender, the head of marketing or head of sales, plus the lender's CEO, with the lender's compliance lead and General Counsel reached as the veto holders. This is an L, multi-channel launch: a new product entering a defined B2B market, but one lender's pilot doesn't yet justify an XL campaign. Jan 6, 2027 is a release to the pilot lender, not the launch; the launch moment follows the day-90 read of the A/B test in April 2027, and only if Nomi clears the ship bar (at least +25 pts on urgent requests answered with a reviewed asset within 1 business day, with no rise in compliance issues). Channels: owned, founder-led outreach to lender contacts, organic LinkedIn campaigns, and the Nomi website with the clickable demo; earned, the pilot lender's case study and reference calls, PR with mortgage trade press such as HousingWire, and a speaking slot at the Digital Mortgage conference; paid, full-funnel UGC ads on X driving demo requests, because lender CEOs are active there, and a CEO who wants Nomi collapses the slow pace of enterprise sales. Enablement: a buyer one-pager built on referral revenue, not cost savings; a compliance pack for the lender's GC (audit trail, ruleset, 10% sampling audit); the pilot case study; an objection sheet ("Will our GC approve AI review?", "Will flyers look alike?", "Is this cutting designers?"); and an LO onboarding guide. Owners: Jessica Manna (founder) owns the launch plan, founder-led sales, PR, and speaking; the designer owns the case study, one-pager, and ad creative; the compliance advisor owns the compliance pack; the engineers own lender onboarding and usage tracking. Budget: $5,000 a month, mostly time and content production, approved before launch. Timeline: Phase 1 (Jan 6 to early April 2027), the pilot lender runs the A/B test; Phase 2 (mid-April 2027), the case study, PR, outreach, and ads go live together; Phase 3 (May onward), we onboard new lenders and track LO activation and compliance at each one. Bad signal: demos and pilot requests but no signed contracts, which points to pricing, the value proposition, or the compliance veto, not lead volume. Likely decision: double down if lenders sign and their top producers activate; iterate on messaging if demos stall with buyers; pivot to compliance-led selling if the GC is the blocker.
