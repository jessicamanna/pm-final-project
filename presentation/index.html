The problem hook

Today no path is both fast enough to keep the door open and safe enough to carry the lender’s name.

When a referral partner says “send me something,” a loan officer has about a day to show she’s the professional worth sending business to. Her lender’s process takes about a week.

Value proposition

For retail branch loan officers, starting with top producers, Nomi delivers professional, on-brand, compliance-reviewed collateral that looks like theirs, fast enough to use while the door is still open.

Open risks from the cold read
  • Will lenders buy Nomi, and who at the lender signs?
  • Will lenders’ compliance teams trust AI review on rates and co-branding?
  • Could templates that look alike recreate the problem faster?

The case rests on referral revenue, not time saved: a buyer hears “time saved” as “cut designers.”

Why not revenue?

The P90 time from lead to funding for a purchase loan is 164 days, and only about 7% of referrals close and fund. It stays the lagging North Star, reviewed after the test.

Why not a pre/post pilot?

No control group. Losing two production staff alone moved urgent on-time delivery from 34% to 19%.

The honest limit

With 30 LOs, the test can confirm a large lift but not a small one. Control LOs get Nomi at the read date if it ships.

The go/no-go call follows a rule written now, not how the results look later.

Ship

Nomi beats the control by at least 25 pts at p < 0.05 and the compliance issue rate stays within its boundary at day 90. Roll out to all of the lender’s top producers, with new LOs next.

Iterate

Nomi is ahead, but by less than 25 pts. Sharpen the chat brief or templates, or add a second lender for more sample, and re-run.

Investigate

The lift clears 25 pts but the guardrail breaks, or results get worse for one branch or partner type. Find who was harmed before any rollout.

Kill

Nomi shows no improvement over the control, or does worse.

Not run yet. The test is planned with one lender: 30 top producers randomized 15/15 on day 0, with the read on day 90 and no results reviewed before then.

The PM’s job isn’t to run the most academic experiment. It’s to choose evidence that’s good enough for the decision in front of you, be honest about what it can’t prove, and move.

The aha moment, while designing the experiment

Friction

Nomi doesn’t lend itself to ruthless, data-driven experimentation. A purchase loan can take 164 days from lead to funding, and only about 7% of referrals close. And no CEO wants an academic study: they want it rolled out to everyone, fast.

Key learning

I’m a marketer who has worked in product-led organizations. The rigor of designing before building, and working through every step instead of jumping to a vibe-coded build, changed what I was creating and who I was solving for.
